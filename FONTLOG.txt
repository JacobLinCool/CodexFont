Clear Hand TC FONTLOG

Version 0.100 — public preview — 2026-07-27

- Expanded the encoded repertoire to exactly 9,120 Unicode scalars:
  6,060 Han characters and 3,060 non-Han characters. The Han set covers all
  4,808 characters in Taiwan's Ministry of Education common-character table.
- Added 3,043 visible non-Han glyphs covering Latin, Greek, IPA, Bopomofo,
  kana, digits, punctuation, combining marks, fullwidth forms, mathematical
  and technical symbols, arrows, geometric symbols, box drawing, and block
  elements.
- Added 17 independently approved semantic-empty spacing and control glyphs
  with explicit zero-ink evidence and Unicode-appropriate metrics.
- Added 93 independently approved, unencoded vertical alternates selected by
  the OpenType `vert` and `vrt2` features.
- Generated every new visible outline from an original white-background,
  black-ink imagegen raster. No external font outline, authored fallback,
  alias, or programmatically drawn replacement enters the release.
- Normalized only the em-boundary placement and Unicode-defined fractions of
  image-generated box-drawing and block-element topology; the visible
  contours remain derived from the approved raster originals.
- Required exact source identity, topology, edge-contact, small-size coverage,
  zero micro-edge, and zero acute-micro-turn checks before manual review and
  release authorization.
- Reviewed every visible non-Han glyph at 12, 16, 20, 32, and 56 px in both
  black-on-white and white-on-black proofs.
- Added GPOS kerning and mark/mkmk positioning together with GDEF mark
  classes, while preserving exact horizontal and vertical metrics across all
  shipped formats.
- Shipped matching TrueType-quadratic TTF, OTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 artifacts with
  an exact 9,120-entry cmap and a 9,214-entry glyph order including `.notdef`
  and the 93 vertical alternates.
- Bound the release report to the frozen Han, non-Han, and vertical
  repertoires; all raster, descriptor, review, authorization, proof, and
  artifact SHA-256 evidence; and deterministic rebuild results.

Scope boundary

Version 0.100 is the 9,120-codepoint public preview, not the complete
18,071-codepoint repertoire described by PROMPT.md. Unsupported characters
must be rendered by an explicitly selected fallback family and are not counted
as Clear Hand TC coverage.

Mew Note TC FONTLOG

Version 0.1 Preview — 2026-07-29

- Added the original Mew Note TC family with rounded gel-pen strokes and
  occasional identity-safe cat-tail terminals.
- Shipped 9,088 encoded codepoints: 6,028 Han and 3,060 non-Han, plus 93
  unencoded vertical alternates.
- Covered all 4,808 Ministry of Education common Han characters, all 93
  hiragana, and all 96 katakana.
- Withheld two atomic 16-glyph Han batches because one scalar in each could
  not be proven by the frozen exact-cmap identity catalog.
- Generated and independently reviewed every shipped visible glyph from
  family-local raster originals. All admitted outlines have zero micro edges
  and zero acute micro turns.
- Added merged-font proofs at 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 36, 56, and 72 px in both
  polarities, plus 56 px HarfBuzz vertical-shaping proofs.
